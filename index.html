About AW Supply Co.

A Reliable Partner for Retail Distribution

AW Supply Co. is a Texas-based wholesale distributor specializing in home goods and household products. We work with established brands and emerging product lines to bring quality merchandise to retail channels and online marketplaces nationwide.

Our team brings deep expertise in supply chain logistics, product sourcing, and retail compliance — allowing our partners to move product efficiently and confidently at scale.

We maintain rigorous standards for product quality, documentation, and fulfillment to ensure every order meets the expectations of our retail partners.

Verified Wholesale Distributor
AW Supply Co. operates as a registered business entity in the State of Texas, maintaining full compliance with wholesale distribution standards and retail partner requirements.
Established Supply Chain
Our sourcing network and logistics infrastructure allow us to fulfill orders consistently, with proper documentation, invoicing, and traceability at every step.
Retail-Ready Products
Every product we distribute is vetted for quality, compliance, and market readiness — ensuring our retail partners receive merchandise that meets platform and consumer expectations.
